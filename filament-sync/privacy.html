Filament Sync privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Filament Sync is a personal script used only by its author. This policy describes what it does with Google user data.

Data accessed

With the Google Sheets scope, the script reads and writes one spreadsheet owned by its author: filament brand, type, colour, spool weight, remaining weight, status and a spool ID. It does not access any other Google data.

Use and storage

The data is used only to keep that spreadsheet in step with the author's own Bambu Lab Filament Manager. The script runs on the author's computer; its OAuth token and a record of the last synced values are stored there, readable only by the author's user account.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Spreadsheet contents are sent only to Bambu Lab's own service, as part of the sync the author asked for.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the local token removes the stored credentials.
